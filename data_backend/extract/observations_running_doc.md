# Observations — running doc

- DLP clause consistently sits on page 3 of BBMP agreements, not page 2 — page 2's "180 Days" is the completion period, not DLP. Missing page 3 gave 5 of 9 works a wrong/blank DLP.
- A single work can have multiple "Agreement" PDFs attached: one civil contract, one PMC (consultant) contract. Filename gives no reliable signal (`Pk1-AGR.pdf` was PMC; `Agreement.pdf` was PMC; neither said "pmc").
- PMC contract amounts (~₹10–40L consultancy fees) are a different order of magnitude from civil contract values (₹10L–17Cr) — mixing them into Track 4 would silently corrupt the estimate-vs-payout comparison.
- Indent numbers are confirmed unique per tender (user-verified) — bare "Indent No. XXXXX" is a usable join key even without year/office qualifiers.
- Same work order and agreement PDF of a work often both print the tender/indent number independently — free corroboration when they agree.
- OCR on "text-layer" PDFs still has real errors: `EE/MLP/TEND` read as `EEIMLPITEND`; one indent number read as `33744` in one doc and `53744` in another for the same work (1-digit OCR flip).
- Two different works (`040-23-000012`, `072-23-000008`) cited the identical tender number and date — one tender notification can legitimately cover multiple work packages. Tender→work is one-to-many, not one-to-one.
- Estimates PDFs are large (22+ page BOQ tables) and contributed zero Track 1/3/4 fields — 744MB of a 1GB fetch for no relevant data. Dropped from fetch scope; Agreement + Tender Documents cover all three tracks.
- ds3 CSV's `documents_detail` filenames often contain literal spaces (`Estimate - 198-23-00005.pdf`). Local filesystem storage requires sanitizing spaces to underscores, but the *remote* download URL needs the real filename with `%20` — conflating the two silently produces 404s.
- ds3 has no ward column; ward must be inferred from `ddoname` (DDO office) or `wcdescription` text, and the two disagree often (46 vs 74 matches for "Horamavu", only 14 overlapping).
- The citydocs metadata-repo `work_orders` table has a clean, 100%-populated `ward_number`/`ward_name` — sourced from the VSS scrape filename, corroborated independently by the job number's first segment and (for one sub-corpus) an in-record `Ward :` text line. Agreement rate between filename and job number: 99.9%.
- ds3 is one row per bill, not per work — a work with 20+ bills appears 20+ times with different `gross` each time; must group by `WO` and sum.
- Live BBMP scans are consistently large (~4MB+ each) and have no text layer (~81% of this corpus) — vision extraction is mandatory, not optional, for most documents.
- Rendering only the first 3 pages at 110 DPI (vs full doc at 200 DPI) cut a 20-work sample from 1,312 pages to 338 with no loss on Track 1/3/4 fields — but this cap is *why* DLP was initially missed on page 3, so the cap and the page-position assumption need to be checked together, not independently.
- Regex on text-layer PDFs is unsafe even when the text is genuinely present: `pdftotext -layout` fragments clauses across column boundaries (e.g. "defect ... liability period is three\nyear" split by unrelated column text in between), so a regex anchor can fail to match a clause sitting right there in the extracted text — silently, with no signal anything was missed. Caught via user spot-check on `007-23-000003`: DLP ("three years") and tender number (`EE/BTP/BBMP/E-PMT/10/2022-23`) were both visibly present and both missed by regex.
- Fix: regex demoted from "authoritative extraction" to "pre-fill hint" only. Every PDF — including the ~13% with a real text layer — is now rendered and actually read; text-layer content is passed alongside as a candidate to confirm, never trusted unread.
- With all 20 sampled works' Agreements actually read: 16/20 DLP confirmed, 7/20 STRONG tender links, 6/20 STRONG Track 4 amounts. Remaining gaps are genuine blockers, not unread pages: one work's Agreement PDFs all 404 (document no longer served), one work's only-found Agreement is PMC (civil copy not in the fetched set), one work's DLP/tender text is confirmed absent after a full read, one work likely needs a page 4 beyond the render cap.
- Same tender number `BBMP/EE/KEN/TEND/09/2022-23` (12.07.2022, Kengeri/Rajarajeshwari Nagar zone) appeared on 4 of the 20 sampled works, all citing the identical Commissioner Office Order `Comm/PR/318/2020-21` for a uniform 3-year DLP — repeated, not coincidental. Strong evidence one tender notification can legitimately span many job codes zone-wide; Track 1 join logic must allow tender→work as one-to-many.
- PMC/DPR consultancy agreements turned out to be the norm, not the exception, on multi-package works — several works had 2-4 Agreement PDFs with more than one being a different consultant's contract (3 distinct firms seen: Civil Quality Consultants, Vimos Technocrats, Sagar Consultants/Team For You). Content-based `is_pmc` detection correctly separated civil from consultancy on every case encountered.
- One work (`143-23-000002`) had an Agreement PDF with DLP/amount figures that don't reconcile with its own civil contract's Rs.21+ crore total (a much smaller ~Rs.4.4L range) — flagged as a discrepancy needing reviewer attention rather than silently picked.
- `paid_gross_rupees` (summed across all of a WO's bill rows) can be wildly larger than a single Agreement's `amount_quoted_rupees` when one job code bundles multiple works/packages under one WO (seen up to +15,576% and +463% "variance") — this is a real multi-work-per-WO structural mismatch, not a data error; the payout-vs-contract comparison is unsafe to compute per-WO without first splitting by package.
- ds3's `contractorname` column can point at a PMC/DPR consultancy rather than the executing civil contractor, silently and with no flag — confirmed on 2 of 5 works in the second batch (`002-23-000003` → Sri Sai Consultants; `003-23-000006` → Rudraprasad Consultants), both cases where the correct civil contractor was only found by matching the work *description* text across several same-job-number Agreement PDFs, not by trusting the CSV's contractor field.
- A distinct, recurring misfiling pattern: a small, unrelated maintenance/desilting/tractor-hire Agreement (different contractor, different DLP, ~1/100th the contract value) turns up filed under the same job-number folder as a larger roads-and-drains package. Seen in 3 of 5 works in the second batch (`002-23-000003`, `009-23-000003`, `003-23-000006`) — frequent enough to be a property of how ds3/the source portal groups attached documents, not random noise. Handled per extraction rule 7 (record, don't resolve) — these must not be picked as the civil contract for Track 3/4.
- The text/vision triage heuristic (`TEXT_LAYER_MIN_CHARS`) was fooled by a scanned PDF whose only "text layer" was a repeated scanner-app watermark ("Scanned by TapScanner" × 33 lines = 757 chars, past the threshold, zero real content) — routed to the text branch and would have silently produced an empty extraction. Fixed by also requiring a minimum count of distinct real words, not just character count.
- One firm (Rudraprasad Consultants) held *both* the DPR-preparation and PMC-supervision contracts for the same package (`003-23-000006`) — at `009-23-000003` the same two roles were split across two different firms (Rudraprasad for DPR, Mecadez for PMC). Both arrangements occur; `is_pmc` detection must not assume one consultant = one role.
- DLP is not always a flat single-clause statement: one Agreement (`009-23-000003`'s misfiled Suresh contract) stated a two-tier DLP by work type (1 year for asphalt ≤20mm thick, 3 years for civil works and thicker asphalt) in a single clause — a form not seen elsewhere in this project.
